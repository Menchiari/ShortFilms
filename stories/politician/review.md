## MAJOR

N1 [MAJOR] S05 SH120 to S06 SH060
Problem: His posture contradicts itself through the rebuilt sequence. He is standing, then on the ground with no fall written, then "thrown to the ground".
Evidence: S05 SH120 "He gets up from the asphalt." S06 SH010 slug "looking down at him", action "On the ground, he points the small nickel pistol at them". S06 SH060 "the crowd hits him like a wave and throws him to the ground." SH020 and SH050 do not say whether he is standing.
Effect: Leonard's wave ("catapulted to the ground") only works on a standing man. Each S06 shot is a separate generation; with posture missing, Seedance will choose a posture per shot.
Direction: Is he standing or down when he fires, and is it the same in all five S06 shots before the wave?

N2 [MAJOR] S06 SH020, SH040 through S09 SH110
Problem: The number of dead, and where each one lies, is not defined. Yet "the dead stay where they fell" is a rule across roughly 15 separately generated shots.
Evidence: S06 SH020 "Shots 2 to 6 of 8. Every man he hits drops where he is", "Another, and another, closer and closer together, until he is firing at one after another." S06 SH040 "Shots 7 and 8 of 8, off screen" (whether they hit is not said). S07 SH080 "A few seconds later, another man drops." S08 SH040 "Some fall while they run." The bible still says two kills.
Effect: Without a count and a position for each kill, bodies will appear and vanish between S06 SH060, S07 SH010/SH080, S08 SH040 and S09 SH010/SH020/SH110.
Direction: How many of the five shots in the side view drop someone, and do shots 7 and 8 hit anyone? Should one body map (his kills at the corner, the sniper's across the square) be the reference for every later shot?

N3 [MAJOR] S06 SH010 note, S06 SH050 (carried over from the d7 review's N7, still unresolved)
Problem: The click depends on how the chosen pistol behaves when empty, and nobody has checked it.
Evidence: SH010 "Gun: Walther PPK/S, nickel, 7 in the magazine + 1 chambered = 8"; SH050 "Click. Nothing."
Effect: To be researched, not stated as fact: if this pistol's slide locks open after the last round, there is no click, and the open slide shows in the close. The 7+1 capacity also depends on a calibre the draft never names.
Direction: Check hold-open and capacity for a named calibre. If the slide locks open, which "small portable gun, like someone like him would do" clicks with 8 rounds?

N4 [MAJOR] S07 SH050, SH070; S09 SH010
Problem: Leonard's change (the pipe man is "same fat guy at the beginning") depends on the audience recognising him. None of the S07 or S09 views is set up to make that happen.
Evidence: S07 SH050 "POV, upside down"; SH070 "LOW, looking up... The pipe rises high against the sky" (a figure against the sky). S09 SH010 "in prompts, a sleeping man." In S04 he has no visual marker beyond "a big, robust FAT PERSON".
Effect: If the audience misses the match, the payoff (S04 "in front of everyone", the pipe, the spit in S09) is lost.
Direction: What one thing seen in S04 (clothing, an object, his build) carries him through S07 and S09? Which S07 frame shows his face the right way up?
