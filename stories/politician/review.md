BRAINTRUST REVIEW: Politician, shooting script draft 7 (staging and edit pass)
(Earlier reviews are in git history.)

Stage reviewed: draft 7, 104 shots, 6:11. Lenses: Garland, Cameron (geography), Kubrick (research).
Notes are not applied: each one waits for Leonard's approval. The fixes listed add about 25 to 35 s.

BLOCKING
- N1 S09 SH020: the briefcase lies in the street where he threw it, on the far side of the car from his
  corner, and the README lands the helicopter south-west of the car, in front of him in this frame. Fix:
  the trip and the throw happen at the mouth of the west street; the landing moves so it is behind him
  as seen from the case; hold the shot 18 to 20 s or cut once mid-walk.
- N2 S10 SH010: the car faces north and the sun sets in the west, so the helicopter cannot fly into the
  sunset through the windshield. Fix: turn the car to face west and land the helicopter east of it.
- N3 S06 SH040: draft 7 adds a third kill ("A runner falls."). Leonard wrote two. Fix: shots 4 and 5
  miss. Keep five shots fired before "Four...".

MAJOR
- N4 S09 SH010: with his view of the bodies cut, the near smile comes before he sees the dead. Fix: bodies
  soft in the long-lens foreground and on the far sidewalk; a slow look along the street before the smile.
- N5 S06 SH110 to SH120: the choice between the crowd and the bullet never shares a frame. Fix: in the
  last second of SH110 the muzzle rises into the soft foreground; cut on that move.
- N6 S06: a crowd that swallowed him stands 40 m off 11 s later, and a pocket pistol hits at 30 to 40 m.
  Research: the distance an untrained shooter hits anything with a pocket pistol; scale the shot to it.
- N7 S06 SH120: the PP/PPK family may lock the slide open after the last round: no click, an open slide
  in a frontal close. Research: check the PPK/S; if it locks open, pick a pistol that does not and keep 8.
- N8 S09 SH010: a medium close on a man on the ground loses the stain, the legs and the body. Fix: the
  stain reads once he leans back with his lap in frame, or widen to a medium; the body is the pipe man,
  pipe beside him; 18 to 20 s.
- N9 S08 SH020: from the back of the cabin Roger's smile cannot be read. Fix: a 2 s close of the smile, or
  a camera forward of him.
- N10 Durations: S03 SH050 18 to 20 s (and "Not here, no..." may not carry to the balcony); S09 SH110 8 s;
  S05 SH110 10 s; S01 SH030 4 s.

MINOR
- N11 The holds sit on travel shots, not on the violence. Fix: hold the lips through the whole count with
  the shots heard, one cut to the wide from behind; S07 SH010 and S08 SH040 6 to 8 s; S02 SH010 6 s.
- N12 S03 to S10 are continuous, yet the light goes from day to sunset. Fix: one late-afternoon sun, low in
  the west; it touches the horizon only in S10.
- N13 S04: the friend as the man from the bar cannot be read as staged. Fix: show him get up in SH060 and
  come round the corner in SH110, or drop the assumption.
- N14 Elements: B from S05 SH110, C from S07 SH030, D at S09 SH010.
- N15 S05 SH110 and S07 SH010 show the same image. Fix: S05 SH110 at ground level, car behind, street light
  in frame; S07 SH010 high.
- N16 S10: the rotor wash is gone by the time the camera comes down. Fix: the bobblehead soft at the bottom
  of frame from the start, nodding hard at lift-off.
- N17 S01: the billboard and the tear gas are new and untagged; the policeman's pull-back was lost. Fix:
  tag them PROPOSED or use his items (looting, people escaping); restore the pull-back.
- N18 Header: "Not anymore." is Leonard's, not proposed.

Assumptions: "he looks back" is the old man: keep. The friend is the man from the bar: not readable as
staged (N13). The helicopter "holds still": already watching, or just arrived? Leonard's call.

Keep: the count arithmetic (8 rounds, the warning shot forgotten, five shots before "Four...", "One." after
shot 8).

Open for Leonard: car facing west (N2); helicopter watching or arriving; the smoker in frame or only the
camera position; two kills or three (N3).
