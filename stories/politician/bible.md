## Story triangle position; declared structure breaks
Closed ending; external conflict; single active protagonist until the gun is empty; linear; causal; consistent reality. Archplot at short scale.
Declared break: viewpoint switch [MINE, Q12].
1. Element broken: single viewpoint. The film follows him until the helicopter, then shows the helicopter (Roger, the lady) before he sees it.
2. Triangle movement: small, toward multiple viewpoints; still Archplot.
3. Audience loses: being inside his fear at the moment of rescue.
4. Audience gains: the power above him becomes visible; the genre turns to political thriller.
5. Replaces it: the camera returns to him for the ending ("The ending needs to stay with the politician" [MINE]).
