## The idea, verbatim

I'm writing a new short film. Not too long, 2-3 minutes probably. It's a test film, but I want it to be very cool. I'm picturing some surreal but hyper realistic scenario set in a city that resembles either southern Italy or Egypt, something like slums, outskirts, the part of the city that you're afraid of usually, sketchy as fuck.
I would like to get some inspiration from the egypt revolution I was in during 2012 in Tahrir square, but not in egypt. It should be a very confusing place, unclear, generic, it could really be anywhere. I would want them to speak English with a strong accent, so that we don't know where anyone is from. The main character, the politician, should also have a bit of an accent, but should be clearly america, the land of capitalism, where everything is possible, including what I'm about to paste. The goal is to make people feel that gut feeling of nausea and rage for the shit that is happening to the world today, without creating anything too explicit. I wrote this a few times, so I'll paste both parts, and I need to make the ending too.

EXT. DESERTED TOWN - CONT.
The buildings around him are tall, but nobody seems to be around. It’s right after lunch, which means that most are either still eating or napping. You can hear the main news channel echoing between the apartments’ cheap televisions.
The old man glances next to him. His BRIEFCASE rests on the front seat of his car.
OLD MAN
We’ll get there, don’t worry... Wait till they see what I was able to...what--? No, no no no...
The car engine pops. The motor slows down, then stops running.
The car slows down, easing to a full stop. The fuel tank is now completely empty.
The last clap of the dying car’s engine echoes through the buildings.
OLD MAN
Not here, no...
He peeks around. The streets are still empty. He picks up the briefcase and exits the car, leaving the door open.
He walks, fast, steadily increasing his speed, trying not to make any noise or attract any attention. His breath gets louder, heavier. His eyes widen at each extra step, checking around him constantly.
On his left, he notices a small group of people. They are minding their own business.
He looks right, but before he realizes, he bumps into a big, robust FAT PERSON.
The fat person and the old man look at each other for a moment. The old man is terrorized, but tries to not make it obvious; the fat person is very curious. He can’t quite figure it out, until his eyes widen with excitement.
FAT PERSON
This can’t be. Is it really you?
The old man covers his face.
OLD MAN
I don’t know who you think I am, but I have no idea who you are. I don’t know you!
FAT PERSON
IT IS YOU. But what are you doing here, all alone... nobody around to baby-sit you...?
The fat person’s friend behind them grabs his phone and starts texting.
FAT PERSON
You have RUINED so many lives. What should I do to you now that no one is around to save your delicate, wrinkly old ass?
The fat person grabs the old man from his shirt’s collar.
FAT PERSON
So... Is that what you like to do with our tax money? Give me one reason why I shouldn’t rip your guts open right now, in front of everyone.
Around him, people stare out of their windows.
The old man shrinks and slides off the fat person’s grip. The fat person lets him go for a moment, then he turns towards his friend.
FRIEND
Are you stupid!? Go after him. IT’S REALLY HIM!
As the old man walks away, voices rise from all around. People stare from outside their apartment windows, some shout at him. What once was silence, is now many voices from the buildings getting louder and louder.
VOICES AROUND
It’s him? Oh god, it’s really him!
The old man wipes some sweat from his forehead while increasing his walk speed. He peeks behind him. The two friends start chasing him, first walking, then at a faster pace. Around them more and more people gather, all following him.
The old man is now running. A whole crowd is chasing him now. The crowd is faster than him. They are angry. They have almost reached him. As he runs, the briefcase opens.
Money comes out of the briefcase and flies all around him, dragged by the wind and raining onto the crowd.
Everyone goes crazy and drops on the floor to grab as much cash as they can. The old man tries desperately to close his briefcase, but struck by panic, he decides to throw it away to get rid of the crowd instead.
VOICE
Don’t let him get away!!!
The old man grabs his phone and presses the screen. The sweat on his fingers makes it hard for him to unlock his device and call for help.
Finally, he dials the number.
OLD MAN
It’s me! I have the money! The car broke down so I’m on foot and I need you to come get me, fast! I’m slightly south from where we were supposed to meet, the add--
A sharp, heavy rock strikes his head between the phone and his ringing ear, causing him to lose balance and roll onto the sidewalk of the dusty street. Blood quickly rushes onto his face.
The crowd has almost reached him. The yells are unbearably loud.
A gunshot echo freezes everyone in sight.
OLD MAN
STAY BACK!!!
The old man is now holding his revolver up high; he’s breathing heavily. He points the gun towards the crowd. They all take a step back.
OLD MAN
Don’t get close! Go away!
Some of the crowd walks back. The tough ones, the strongest, the bravest, the ones who have no fear step forward.
The crowd has now turned into a smaller, ruthless and violent angry mob. It doesn’t run, it walks slowly towards the old man, savoring in advance the inevitable without any fear.
The old man shoots, one person dies. He shoots again, another person drops dead.
The crowd doesn’t stop. But now rather than walking is aggressively sprinting towards him.
He keeps shooting until the loud gunshots turn into short metallic clicks. Shaking and soaked in sweat, he points the gun to himself, realizing that he is now out of bullets.
He stares at the inevitable destiny that lies in front of his eyes, as the remaining crowd eventually reaches him.
Seen from afar, the old man is first surrounded by the angry mobsters, then vanishes inside the small crowd as he gets hit and kicked while crawling onto the floor.
Someone grabs a rope and ties his feet, pulls it and hangs him upside down from a street light.
The old man, face bruised like a balloon, blood dripping from his ear and his lips, can barely open his eyes. With some effort, he opens one of his eyelids, just before seeing a metal pipe hitting him on his face.

His brains drop onto the sidewalk.

The small group of people that is now around him realizes what just happened. Scared, they quickly disperse, leaving the body hanging from its feet on the side of the street.
The town is once again silent.

I like the car speeding at the beginning. I like the riots as an intro. I'm not sure about what they are fighting for or how to make all that super realistic. I need it to be much more believable. The main character needs to be the symbol of everything that everyone hates in politics, but no references to real figures (I don't want a Trump, for example, that shit gets old - everyone can get corrupt and there are way worse political figures out there. I was thinking someone like berlusconi could be an example, but that also gets old. Generic, hateable, dispicable, even if he's trying to be good looking like some of these tech assholes, could be fat, could be full of botox or I have no fucking clue, this part can be explored later).
I don't like that his head gets splattered and brains go all over the sidewalk. It's unnecessay satisfaction. This is what we would probably expect, what would happen in real life, but what I'm thinking is a twist. When you're about to see the guy being killed, probably after a swing or two, we move into Kojima style or Captain Phillips ending type of Tom Clancy style of realistic political almost sci-fi but still super realistic, the kind of tech that we all know exists but nobody can see it yet. Why is this politician so important we do not know. Is he even a politician? Why are some higher up people trying to save him, we don't know, but at some point something happens.
The guy that is about to give the final blow with the metal rod gets shot in the face buy a sniper. The crowd moves back. Then more shots, and from afar, from a fancy helicopter (we need the model name and number of course), we hear multiple shots, killing multiple people while others disperse. We could rip some videos from real riots or I could have you make a little crowd simulator with physics so each person is like a ball and we have the crowd effect that works well, or we could make the crowd in Unreal Engine or Blender, not sure what would work best, but that part could be tricky. Let's do one step at the time though.
The crowd disperses, some get killed, while they run, some more get killed just for fun. In the helicopter, the guy shooting is smiling, enjoying the process, the business lady behind him tells him to stop. "That's enough Roger". The guy acts like his candy was just taken away from him for an instant, then gets back into serious mode, and aims for the rope. A shot. The rope snaps. The politician guy drops on the ground, yelling, pissing himself, looking at the people around him dead, but then you see that he's a person with guts. That's not the kind of stuff that gets to him. He's aware of the damage he's doing to the world, and he doesn't care. He spits on the dead. He gets up and goes back to the briefcase while the helicopter lands.
At this point I have no idea how to do this, but I'm picturing the business lady getting off the helicopter and having a short exchange of words with the politician. She's got him, and he needs to get back to work. Why do they still want him after his reputation went so much to shit? This is the clue part of the movie. It can remain obscure as long as it's clear that it makes sense in a way. They hop on the helicopter and move on, ready to fuck the world up.

That final part... we really need to figure it out. It's the message of the movie, and I want something really fucked up, something that makes us think: oh shit. Of course. It could be as simple as "let's get back to work" or "there's a lot of work left to be done". Things like that.
It could end with the helicopter flying into the distance, in the sky.

The ending is unclear. Weak, too simple. I'm overthinking it. Let's try to think of something together. So far this is what I've got. I think you get a bit of the direction. The goal is to get a final script in the end.
