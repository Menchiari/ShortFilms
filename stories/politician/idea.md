## The idea, verbatim

I'm writing a new short film. Not too long, 2-3 minutes probably. It's a test film, but I want it to be very cool. I'm picturing some surreal but hyper realistic scenario set in a city that resembles either southern Italy or Egypt, something like slums, outskirts, the part of the city that you're afraid of usually, sketchy as fuck.
I would like to get some inspiration from the egypt revolution I was in during 2012 in Tahrir square, but not in egypt. It should be a very confusing place, unclear, generic, it could really be anywhere. I would want them to speak English with a strong accent, so that we don't know where anyone is from. The main character, the politician, should also have a bit of an accent, but should be clearly america, the land of capitalism, where everything is possible, including what I'm about to paste. The goal is to make people feel that gut feeling of nausea and rage for the shit that is happening to the world today, without creating anything too explicit. I wrote this a few times, so I'll paste both parts, and I need to make the ending too.
