# Reference clips, draft 10

One clean clip per 3D shot of animatic d10 (`<SCENE>_<SHOT>.mp4`, 24 fps, 1120x480): no labels, no subtitles, no faces
(close shots rendered with `SHAPE_SLITS=0`). Text-card shots have no clip. Shot ids follow draft 10 (no renumbering:
S04 SH135 and S06 SH025 are new; S05 SH040 to SH060 and SH100 are cut).

Use: the prompt names each clip and its one job ("a motion diagram only: the camera angle and the timing and paths of
the people"). Test S05 SH020 showed that a clip with the grey set gets copied as a fake 3D model: for crowd and fall
shots use the motion-only clip in `../refs_d10_motion/` (people on plain ground, no set), and describe the place in words.
