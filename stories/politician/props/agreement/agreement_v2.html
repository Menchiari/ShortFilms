ARTICLE 18
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 18.1Irrevocability. This Agreement and the rights granted hereunder are irrevocable and shall survive any change of government, constitution, legislature or public order in the Territory. No subsequent law, decree or referendum shall bind the Concessionaire.

Section 18.2Waiver of Sovereign Immunity. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Grantor irrevocably waives any immunity, on the grounds of sovereignty or otherwise, from suit, jurisdiction, attachment or execution in respect of itself, its assets, its revenues and the Population Data.

Section 18.3Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by private arbitration seated outside the Territory. The proceedings, any award and the existence of this Agreement shall be kept strictly confidential.

Section 18.4Survival. Sections 11.2 (Utilities), 12.1 (Pricing Engine), 12.3 (Normalization), 13.4 (Clearance of Designated Development Zones), 14.1 (Autonomous Administration), 14.6 (No Personal Liability), 15.2 (Data Collateral) and 16.3 (Market Positions) shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for any reason whatsoever.

Section 18.5Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior understandings, public statements and electoral commitments of the Grantor.

Section 18.6Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

In Witness Whereof, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
