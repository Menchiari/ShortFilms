MASTER CONCESSION, DATA COLLATERAL AND AUTONOMOUS ADMINISTRATION AGREEMENT
between THE GOVERNMENT OF THE TERRITORY (the "Grantor") and QUELLMAR CONTINUITY HOLDINGS S.A. (the "Concessionaire")

Article 11.2 (Utilities). The Grantor irrevocably assigns to the Concessionaire, for a term of ninety-nine (99) years, all rights in water, energy, fuel distribution, ports and land registries within the Territory, free of any public service obligation.

Article 12.1 (Pricing). Prices of fuel and staple goods shall be set daily by the Pricing Engine of the System and shall not be subject to review, appeal or publication.

Article 12.3 (Normalization). Upon execution of this Agreement, the Pricing Engine shall reduce retail fuel prices to the level required to restore public order, and shall credit the reduction to the Grantor's signatory.

Article 13.4 (Designated Development Zones). Residents of Designated Development Zones shall vacate by the Clearance Date. Occupancy after the Clearance Date constitutes trespass against the Concessionaire.

Article 14.1 (Autonomous Administration). All Allocative Determinations, including credit, residency, rationing, medical priority and the deployment of security personnel, are delegated to the Autonomous Administration System (the "System"). Determinations of the System are final.

Article 14.6 (No Personal Liability). No natural person shall be liable, civilly or criminally, for any Determination of the System or for any consequence thereof.

Article 15.2 (Data Collateral). The biometric, financial, behavioral and communications data of the population of the Territory are pledged to the Concessionaire as collateral for the Grantor's obligations. Indicators of dissent may be used as an input to risk scoring.

Article 16.3 (Market Positions). Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Concessionaire or its affiliates from holding positions adverse to the securities, currency or credit of the Grantor, including short positions.

Article 17.1 (Protection of Designated Persons). The Concessionaire's security contractors are authorized to use all necessary force for the protection of Designated Persons. The Grantor waives all claims arising therefrom.

Article 17.2 (Costs). All costs of such protection shall be borne by the Grantor and recovered through general taxation.

Article 18.1 (Irrevocability). This Agreement survives any change of government, constitution or public order. The Grantor waives sovereign immunity. Disputes shall be settled by private arbitration, in closed session.
