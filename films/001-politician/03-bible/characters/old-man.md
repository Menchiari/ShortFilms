# The old man

Role: protagonist. One sheet for every shot he is in, so he stays the same person from S02 to S10.
Look comes from Leonard's elements; this sheet holds what the images cannot: voice, behavior,
acting register, and the order of his states. Written 2026-11-17 from char_politician
(`refs/char_politician.webp`), the story bible and Leonard's direction for S03 SH020.

## Identity (locked)
- Age: late fifties [inferred from the image].
- Build: heavy-set, thick neck, broad shoulders, a belly the suit barely closes over.
- Face: square and heavy, deep vertical lines between the brows, small hard eyes under a low
  brow, a straight nose, a thin mouth turned down, grey-brown stubble, flushed sweaty skin,
  enlarged pores.
- Hair: brown going grey, receding, thin on the crown, sweat-slicked and messy, strands sticking
  to the forehead and temples.
- Hands: thick fingers, no rings.
- Voice: low, gravelly, tired. American with a slight accent nobody can place (bible). Short
  phrases, half swallowed.

## Wardrobe
Charcoal suit (jacket on), white shirt with rust-brown stains and dust speckles, rust tie, black
leather belt with a brass buckle, scuffed black leather shoes. Wardrobe never changes; only its
damage grows with the states below.

## Acting register (Leonard, 2026-11-17)
- Subtle. Nothing is shown, everything is held in.
- The heat and the stress are getting into his head, like a fever, and his determination is
  still stronger: he will get out of there alive.
- Rage burns in him all the time. He is long used to swallowing it and staying calm, whatever
  mess he is in, and he is always in one.
- When he speaks, he talks to himself: a grouchy, exhausted mumble, teeth clenched, the jaw barely
  opening, the lips moving little. Never projected, never performed for anyone.
- His eyes keep working (the road, the threat, the case). Never a blank stare.
- In prompts, his ACTING TASK MOTIVE line is always: his whole life has been one fight after
  another, it has been eating him from the inside for years, and he has never once stopped; what
  he carries in the briefcase has to reach the other side.

## Continuity locks
- One man, the same face, build and suit in every shot. The briefcase is always his.
- Sweat grows through S02 to S05; dust from S04; blood from S05 (elements carry it).
