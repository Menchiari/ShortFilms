# Style: Politician

The voice of this film, decided with the director during analysis. The look line goes word for
word into every shot prompt; the rest shapes how the prompts are written. Never copied from
another film.

## Look line (in every prompt)

S02 to S10 [ASSUMPTION, 2026-11-17, from the request 011 location images]:
`Look: 35mm film, anamorphic, hazy afternoon sun, warm ochre and brown palette, deep brown shadows, dust in the air, fine film grain, documentary realism, not CGI.`

## Light
- One sun for the whole town, low in the west (crossroads README). Hazy afternoon S02 to S05,
  warming S06 and S07, sunset S08 to S10. Every prompt says where the sun is relative to the
  camera and what it does on faces.
- Haze and dust soften the sun in every exterior. Shadows deep brown, never black. Highlights
  warm cream, never pure white (palette card).
- Inside the car: no artificial light. Faces get the sun through the windows plus warm bounce
  from the dashboard, the seats and the sunlit road. Exposure for the face; windows bright and
  hazy, the outside still readable.
- The main street runs north [ASSUMPTION: the car reaches the crossroads facing north], narrow,
  walled in by tall blocks. In the car (Leonard, 2026-11-17): always in the blocks' shade, a wall
  always beside him, one street ahead; only the upper floors catch the sun.
- Exteriors: the sun behind the scene, facing the camera, when the angle allows: hazy backlight,
  dust glowing in it, long soft shadows toward the camera, faces still readable from warm bounce
  (Leonard liked it, S04 SH040 T01, 2026-11-18). Written as an instruction, never left to chance.
- Every prompt carries the lighting paragraph: "Lighting, motivated by the sun only:" where the
  sun is, what is sunlit and what is in shade, the warm bounce on faces, dust in the air, "Deep
  brown shadows that keep their detail, never crushed to black, highlights that roll off softly,
  never clipped."

## Camera grammar
- Wides and observing shots: locked-off on a long lens, the street compressed and hazy behind.
- Paths are written in screen terms (which side, which direction, how big in frame at the
  start and the end). "Toward the camera" alone makes people walk straight into the lens.

## Rhythm
Shot lengths, how cuts happen, where time stretches.

## Prompt density
Dense. Every shot prompt carries, besides the PROMPTING.md skeleton:
- Each reference narrowed to one job: the location element for surfaces only (not its framing,
  people or cars), `palette_afternoon` (or the scene's palette) for color and light only.
- The realism paragraph for every place: "The street is a real, lived-in street photographed on
  location, never blocky or simplified:" then the worn surfaces it holds (cracked plaster,
  facades of different heights, dented shutters, sagging cables, chipped curbs, sand along the
  walls), ending "Nothing looks like a box, a slab or a 3D model." (Leonard, 2026-11-18)
- A task for every person in frame, extras included, so nobody looks into the lens or drifts.
- "No cars" is not enough: name "no parked cars" when the street must be empty.

## Sound grammar
What the generated guide track carries, what stays silent.

## Acting register
How much is said, how much is held back, how characters listen and overlap.

## Style references
Images that set the look (Midjourney or other), in `style-refs/`. Stills and plates: GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst, quality high, 1k (Leonard, 2026-11-17).

## This film never
Gets a set or previz clip as a video reference for a frame filled by place: it comes out as 3D
previz (refs_d11 README). Composition from words, a photoreal start frame, or the line drawing.
Holds a shot where nothing happens, unless the frame is breathtaking (Leonard, 2026-11-17).
Names a real director, film or artist in any prompt: it is a contest entry (Leonard, 2026-11-17).
