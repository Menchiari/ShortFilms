Image 1 is a real gas station price sign. Keep its exact design: the blue panel, the white row labels Regular, Plus and Supreme, each digit on its own separate white-on-blue number panel slid into a metal track, the small white 9/10 panel at the end of each row, the white lines between the rows and the Self label at the bottom.
