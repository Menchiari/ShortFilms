# Music brief: action cue, about 40 s

Short film score cue. The track already starts with an existing synth intro (attached). Your part enters on top of it, takes over with a hard explosion, drives for 21 seconds and ends on one guitar chord slam.

## Attached

- `music_80s-action-synth_T02_end.wav`: the existing intro (dark early 1980s analog synth, drum machine). Only its first 10.98 s are used. Load it at 0:00 of your session.

## Tempo, key, grid

- **80 BPM, 4/4.** One beat = 0.75 s, one bar = 3.00 s.
- **Key: E minor.** Home note low E. Notes E, G, A, B (D as passing note). Stay in E minor: no key change, no modulation.
- **Grid:** set your session to 80 BPM with the first beat at **0.48 s** of the intro file. Downbeats used below: 4.98 s, 10.98 s, 31.98 s. Everything must lock to this grid.

## Timing map (time = seconds from the start of the intro file)

| Time | Bars | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 - 4.98 | | Intro file alone. You play nothing. |
| 4.98 - 10.98 | 2 | **Drum build alone** over the intro: a dry funky hip-hop groove for one bar, then a build (open hats, snare roll, fill) into the downbeat. No other instruments. |

## Sound

- **Reference feel:** the opening of Rage Against the Machine's "Wake Up" (crunchy dry guitar tone, whammy squeals, funky hip-hop drum groove, swagger) crossed with Nine Inch Nails' industrial side (distorted machine drums, noise, aggression).
- **Write an original riff.** Do not copy or quote any riff, melody or part from the references. They describe the feel only.
- Cool, confident, an action hero getting away. Not heavy metal: no double kick, no chugging palm-mute riffs, no shred solos.
- Dry, punchy, close-miked drums. Crunchy amp distortion with a cutting midrange (not fuzz). Growling bass.
- **No repetition:** every 2-bar section is different. No loops.
- One wordless yell only, at 10.98 s. No singing, no lyrics, no rap.

## Deliverables

- **Stems**, same length and start: drums, bass, guitars, synths/noise, yell.
