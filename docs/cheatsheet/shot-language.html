Light

where the light comes from
  • Key the main light
  • Fill softens the shadows
  • Back / rim lifts them off the background
  • Practical a lamp you can see
  • Motivated light with a source
  • Hard / soft crisp or gentle shadows

  • Golden / blue hour low sun / after sunset
  • Say the side: key from screen-left.

Lines & sound

what we hear
  • Language: English, light Neapolitan accent.once, first
  • 2-4s, Marta's line (testing him): {I thought you weren't coming.}time · who · a verb, not a feeling · words
  • Luca keeps his mouth closed.whoever is silent【TITLE】text meant to be seen
  • Diegetic heard in the world
  • Room tone the room's own sound
  • Foley sounds made in post
  • Guide track rough sound

No music in shots (score comes later). Avoid: water, waves, echo · "fanatical", "extremely shocked".

9

Acting task

what they fight for, in this order
Directionshared, unspoken
Motiveeach one's fuel
Goaltheir own fight
Obstacleone crack, it ends
Tacticdone to the partner

Play the direction, never the emotion. The tactic lives in the eyes. Verbs at the partner: test · reassure · corner · dodge · tease · warn · comfort · provoke · plead · charm · probe · stall. Not "sadly", "nervously".

Listeners get a task too. Dead eyes: a task, not catchlights.

10

Cut

how this shot joins the next
J-cut next sound comes first
L-cut sound carries over
  • Cut on action mid-move
  • Match cut shapes rhyme
  • Jump cut time skips
  • Cross-cut two places, in turn
  • Smash cut abrupt contrast
  • Dissolve / fade melt / black

Every take is a new performance: cut to the listener.
